FAQ

Rednote questions, answered.

Is Rednote the same as Xiaohongshu?
Yes. Rednote is the international name commonly used for Xiaohongshu, known in Chinese as 小红书. We use all three terms across the page because international teams and China-market users often search differently.
Do we need a Chinese business entity to start?
Not always. The right setup depends on your category, account type and commercial goals. We assess the most practical launch path before execution begins, including what can be tested before a larger local commitment.
How long does it take to see meaningful results?
Rednote growth is cumulative. A structured pilot needs enough time to test positioning, build a useful content base, seed credible creator conversations and learn from search and engagement data. We define the learning goals and decision points before launch.
Can you manage both content and KOC campaigns?
Yes. Strategy, native Chinese content, creative production, KOC seeding, creator coordination and bilingual reporting can be managed as one connected program instead of splitting the work across unrelated vendors.
Can Rednote work together with WeChat?
Yes. Rednote is often strongest for discovery, research and reputation, while WeChat can support deeper communication, retention and conversion. The right mix depends on your customer journey and China-market setup.

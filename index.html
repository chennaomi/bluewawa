The China Problem

So you want to crack China?

Most brands try one of three things. None of them work.

✕

"We'll just run Google Ads."

Google, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube — all blocked. Your Western playbook doesn't load here.

✕

"We'll translate our English content."

A direct translation in China reads like a robot wrote it. Chinese consumers can smell foreign copy from a mile away (字面翻译 = 翻车).

✕

"We'll hire one KOL and see."

China runs on KOC seeding (素人种草), not one-off influencer drops. One post ≠ a strategy.

You don't need a translator.
You need a localizer.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do we need a Chinese business entity to start?
No. We help brands launch through cross-border (跨境) setups first, then localize when the numbers justify it.
How long until we see results on Rednote?
Real traction in 60–90 days if content + seeding are done right. Anyone promising faster is selling you bots.
What's a realistic budget for a 3-month pilot?
It depends on scope and platform mix. We'll size it together on the call — no surprises, no inflated retainers.
Do we need to be on both Rednote and WeChat?
Not necessarily. We'll recommend the right mix for your stage — usually Rednote first for discovery and reputation, then WeChat to convert and retain through Official Accounts, Mini Programs and private community (私域).
Can you build the tech — Mini Programs, H5, dashboards?
Yes, all in-house. WeChat Mini Programs, H5 campaign pages, membership systems and data dashboards — with WeChat Pay and logistics integration. Content and development sit on the same team, so nothing gets lost between vendors.
How do you report progress and prove ROI?
Regular bilingual reports plus a shared live dashboard — content performance, audience profiles, engagement, leads and conversion funnels. We tie content to outcomes you can read in English, not just likes.

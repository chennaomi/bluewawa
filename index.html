02 / WeChat Concept project

Give interest a next step.

Connect a stay guide to a focused enquiry, so the guest knows what to share and the team has the context to respond.

  • Content outline
  • Menu structure
  • Enquiry flow
Explore the flow & delivery scope

Read: Structure the article around the stay, room choices, transport and useful pre-arrival questions. Use verified property information and approved photos.

Enquire: Ask for intended dates, party size, a preferred contact method and optional requests. Explain how the team will use these details.

Follow up: Acknowledge receipt, then have the team check availability and reply. An enquiry is not a confirmed reservation.

Delivery scope: Chinese article copy, a three-part menu plan, enquiry-page wireframes and a handover checklist. Account setup and a working integration would be scoped separately.

Both samples are self-initiated demonstrations for a fictional business. They illustrate our approach and proposed deliverables, not client engagements or measured campaign results.

The China Problem

So you want to crack China?

Three common shortcuts can leave important gaps in a China launch.

✕

"We'll just run Google Ads."

Google, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube — all blocked. Your Western playbook doesn't load here.

"We'll translate our English content."

Translation alone can miss the questions, references and formats that make content useful to a Chinese audience.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do we need a Chinese business entity to start?
Not in every case. Account options and available functions depend on your entity, market and intended activities. We review the setup before agreeing a launch scope.
How long until we see results on Rednote?
A 90-day pilot provides time to test content, review audience signals and assess relevant enquiries. Results depend on your offer, starting point, scope and follow-up; the pilot is an evaluation period, not a guarantee of growth.
What's a realistic budget for a 3-month pilot?
It depends on scope and platform mix. We'll size it together on the call — no surprises, no inflated retainers.
Do we need to be on both Rednote and WeChat?
Not necessarily. We'll recommend the right mix for your stage — usually Rednote first for discovery and reputation, then WeChat to convert and retain through Official Accounts, Mini Programs and private community (私域).
Can you build the tech — Mini Programs, H5, dashboards?
Yes, all in-house. WeChat Mini Programs, H5 campaign pages, membership systems and data dashboards — with WeChat Pay and logistics integration. Content and development sit on the same team, so nothing gets lost between vendors.
How do you report progress and prove ROI?
Regular bilingual reports plus a shared live dashboard — content performance, audience profiles, engagement, leads and conversion funnels. We tie content to outcomes you can read in English, not just likes.
